These are the printed components of the complete wired remote assembly in the
[product BOM](../../bom.csv). Its Ethernet cable and two M4x8 screws are already
listed as included accessories. The [remote PCB source](../../pcb/remote/remote.sch)
is separate.

Body and knob models are extracted from the original
[complete assembly STEP](https://github.com/researchanddesire/OSSM/blob/41d683fe104f4aef8f478f2ff61269a61e06a183/Hardware/%5BLEGACY%5D%20OSSM%20Printed%20Parts/Source%20files%20for%20modification/OSSM%20-%20Complete%20Assembly%20Jan%2012%202024.step).
The top cover's matching native CAD source has not been recovered.
