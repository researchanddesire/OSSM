## Current actuator clamp

The [actuator-source variant](actuator-pitclamp/README.md) includes the refreshed
upper, motor adapter, dogbones and one-piece lever with integral pivot loop.
It is used by the editable actuator assembly; the Mini/BOM references above
remain available as their matched earlier set.

## Lower pivot

[Lower Pitclamp pivot](lower-pivot/) includes the active/passive configuration,
reinforced print project, and double-arm assembly with mirrored active parts.

These are the two stands the OSSM ships with:

- **1-arm stand:** the single hinge, one active lower PitClamp and the passive
  pivot.
- **2-arm stand:** the double hinge, two arms in series with two active lower
  PitClamps (normal and mirrored) at each joint.

Their parts and quantities are in [the BOM](../../BOM.md#stands).

## Discontinued: pivot-plate stand

The pivot-plate stand is no longer made. Its files stay here for existing
stands:

- [Pivot plate 1](pivot-plate-1.step) (right) and [pivot plate 2](pivot-plate-2.step) (left)
- [Handle spacer](handle-spacer.step), eight per stand

It also used two M6 × 25 adjustable handles and two 38 mm rubber washers. Its
build guides, Stand Build Guide 0.1 and 0.0, are listed on
[ohai.researchanddesire.com/ossm/build-guides](https://ohai.researchanddesire.com/ossm/build-guides).
