# Radiating heart and gear artwork

`rd-conformal-neo-memphis.svg` contains the production material footprints for
the complete usable rear profile. A large pink heart overlays the center of the cyan open-hardware gear and
hides its circular aperture. The original gear path remains underneath,
including its outer contour and original straight tapered-gap angles.
The existing black body is the background; white is a design accent. Concentric segmented bands radiate
from the same center across the full profile toward the hinge. Independently
staggered starts and varied palette colors avoid a shared empty wedge;
the outer contours crop at the keep-in boundary. There are no tools, lips or R+D logo.
The separate tiny hook field stays existing body black.

The gear starts from the first path in the [official OSHWA filled SVG](https://resources.oshwa.org/files/assets/oshw-logo-filled-black.svg),
archived unchanged as `oshw-logo-filled-black.svg`. The composition omits the
wordmark and retains the complete original gear source path. The separate
enlarged heart intentionally overlays and hides its circular aperture; the
straight sides of the original tapered opening keep their original angles. The exact first SVG path is retained as editable source;
manufacturing profiles sample its curves within the recorded tolerance. [OSHWA](https://oshwa.org/resources/open-source-hardware-logo/)
credits Macklin Chaffee (2011) and states that the gear logo is CC-SA, without
specifying a version. This attribution and source license are retained.
The gear is a community symbol; no certification is implied.

## Registration and printing

Artwork coordinates map directly to native X/Z: X is right and Z is up. Rotate
+90° about native X to place the profiles on rear Y0; no overall scale or
translation is applied. Inlays extend inward through Y0..0.6 mm, leaving 2.4 mm
of the original 3 mm rear wall as black backing.

The nominal inward perimeter offset is 2.5 mm with a 0.01 mm tolerance guard.
The field retains the hinge-side return, full narrow tail and separate
hook island; the latter is existing black material. Disjoint material footprints
are actual SVG filled paths, matching the native profiles without unresolved
preview clipping. The unused prior R+D vector mark is removed from this snapshot.
