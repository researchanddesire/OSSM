# 3MF print projects

[PCB cover](pcb-mount-lid-multicolor.3mf): front face down. Filament slots:
**1 black · 2 cyan · 3 white · 4 pink**.

[Clear light pipe](pcb-mount-lid-light-pipe.3mf): print separately in clear PLA
(slot 1), flange down, then insert from the front into the cover’s 4 mm bore.

Saved profile: **P2S, 0.6 mm nozzle, 0.3 mm layers**. Check your printer and
filament mapping before slicing.

Four Ø3.4 mm mounting holes have shallow 90° countersinks, 0.4 mm deep.
