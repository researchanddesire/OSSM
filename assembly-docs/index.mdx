The [bill of materials](/ossm/bom) covers one actuator, extrusion stand and wired
remote, including the Double Double and flange mounts with both ring sizes.

[CAD models](https://github.com/researchanddesire/OSSM/tree/main/hardware/cad)
and [PCB sources](https://github.com/researchanddesire/OSSM/tree/main/hardware/pcb)
are available in the OSSM repository. The
[motor control harness](/ossm/cable-harnesses) has its own WireViz source and
component BOM.
