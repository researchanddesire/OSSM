## CAD models and PCB sources

[Actuator and rail adapter](https://github.com/researchanddesire/OSSM/tree/main/hardware/cad/actuator),
[stand and PitClamp](https://github.com/researchanddesire/OSSM/tree/main/hardware/cad/stand),
[Flange mounts](https://github.com/researchanddesire/OSSM/tree/main/hardware/cad/end-effectors),
and [PCB sources](https://github.com/researchanddesire/OSSM/blob/main/hardware/pcb/README.md)
are available in the repository.

The wired remote is one complete assembly in this BOM. Its printed components
are [one body, one cover and two knobs](https://github.com/researchanddesire/OSSM/blob/main/hardware/cad/remote/README.md);
these are included in the assembly, not additional purchased parts.
